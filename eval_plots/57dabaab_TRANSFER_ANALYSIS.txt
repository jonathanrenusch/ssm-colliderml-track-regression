READING IT
* truth-KF and CKF have essentially the SAME resolution here (1.0-1.1x). On
  single muons with no pileup the CKF's hit assignment is already near-perfect
  once it finds the track; the two differ in EFFICIENCY (69.9% vs 100%), not in
  fit quality. Choosing truth-KF as the reference buys coverage, not a harder
  number -- except that it removes the |d0| > 3 mm seeding blind spot.
* The baseline moved enormously. On legacy p200 the CKF gave d0 65.7 um and this
  SSM beat it 5x. The new truth-KF gives d0 14.24 um -- 4.6x better than the old
  baseline -- because the data is single muons, no pileup, median pT 55 GeV.
* So the old "SSM beats the KF" headline does NOT carry over: even a perfect
  transfer (12.19 um, the in-domain value) would only match the truth-KF on d0,
  and on z0/phi/theta/qop the in-domain SSM values are already 3-14x WORSE than
  this baseline.

CAVEAT ON THE d0 FLOOR
  The truth targets are recomputed analytically from the production vertex and
  momentum assuming a uniform 2 T solenoid (perigee.py), because the campaign
  ships perigee_d0/perigee_z0 as all-NULL. That reconstruction agrees with the
  truth-KF to ~19 um IQR in d0 with zero median, so at the ~14 um level the
  "truth-KF resolution" and the target's own accuracy cannot be separated.
  Treat ~14 um as a floor of the measurement, not a proven KF resolution; any
  sub-15 um claim needs a better truth reference.
