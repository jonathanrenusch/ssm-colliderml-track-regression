Cross-model comparison - iter-3sigma RMSE, no bootstrap

All legacy checkpoints carry the OLD loss norm ranges (d0 +-2.5 mm, z0 +-200 mm).
The new drift_beamspot targets span d0 +-7.07 mm and z0 +-268.8 mm, so those models
physically cannot emit most of the new range. Their new-data d0/z0 numbers are that
ceiling, not model quality - see the in-domain control at the bottom.
